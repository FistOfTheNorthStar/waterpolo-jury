# Privacy policy — Water Polo Jury

Water Polo Jury does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

- The app has no internet permission and makes no network connections.
- Bluetooth is used only to send the scoreboard state (score, clocks, period)
  from the jury-table device to display tablets that you have paired yourself.
  The app does not scan for nearby devices and does not use location.
- The match log (goals, fouls, cap numbers, team names you type in) is stored
  only on the device, and leaves it only when you choose "Export match log"
  and pick an app to share it with.
- There are no ads, analytics or third-party SDKs.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/FistOfTheNorthStar/waterpolo-jury/issues
